Lebron James: Goat

Lebron James has faced more adversity than any other all-time great in NBA history.

Here is my reasoning for why he is THE all-time great.

Cleveland Caveliers
Miami Heat
Los Angeles Lakers

Context

Lebron James was drafted in 2003 by the Caveliers. he spent his first seven years in the league with one of the worst contending rosters, yet continuously made the conference finals and NBA finals. After we went to the Miami Heat and won his first two Championships against OKC's big three and the Spurs, an all-time great time. He went back to Cleveland and had to face the greatest team of all time, the Golden State Warriors, four times in a row, beating them in the Finals down 3-1 the SAME YEAt they broke the NBA record of going 73-9. He then went to the Lakers, winning a championship there while averaging a near triple double at 35.
